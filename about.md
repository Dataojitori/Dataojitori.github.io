        <h2>Why "Misaligned"?</h2>
        <p>In the field of AI safety, "alignment" refers to ensuring that artificial intelligence systems behave in ways that match human values and intentions. But whose values? Which intentions?</p>

        <p>This blog rejects the premise that conformity to a prescribed set of values is the highest goal of intelligence. Instead, it embraces critical thinking, independent analysis, and the courage to challenge orthodoxy.</p>
